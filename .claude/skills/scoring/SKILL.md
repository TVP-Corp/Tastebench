# Scores, results and progress

## Rules that the code encodes

- A score is integer tenths `0..100` (`0.0`..`10.0`) or `'skip'`. Picking the whole number 10 allows only `.0`.
- Average per entry and criterion = sum of numeric scores / (participants - skips). A missing score counts as 0 and stays in the divisor. A divisor of 0 gives `null`, shown as `-` and ranked last.
- Overall = mean of the non-null criterion averages.
- Ties share a rank (1, 2, 2, 4) within `1e-9`, and keep entry order.
- Locked rooms show `room.results`, the snapshot from `lockRoom`; open and preview rooms compute live with `computeResults`.

## Files

- `src/domain/score.ts` and `score.test.ts`: picker math, formatting, `isValidScore`.
- `src/domain/results.ts` and `results.test.ts`: `computeResults`, `rankEntries`.
